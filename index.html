If you only do three things

  1. Get your 30-day planFour questions, and you get the 2 to 4 things that matter for your year.
  2. Write a one-page resumePlain, honest, built around what you've made. Then ask one person to mark it up.
  3. Apply earlyBig-company summer internships open around July to September of the year before. Smaller and local employers hire into spring.
