# Early Programs (freshmen & sophomores)

[⬅ Back to the main page](README.md) · 0 open · updated 2026-11-10

Postings that say freshmen or sophomores can apply, or programs built for them. The grey line under each title is the sentence from the posting, so you can check it.

Big-company programs like Google STEP, Microsoft Explore and Meta University aren't read by this board yet. Check their student career pages directly in the fall.

**Click a job title to open the company's application page.**

**Tags.** 🛂✅ the posting says it sponsors visas · 🛂❌ it says it doesn't · 🚫 it says it won't take CPT/OPT · 🇺🇸 limited to U.S. citizens, green-card holders or U.S. persons (often ITAR) · 🔒 security clearance · 🏛 government or national-lab role, often U.S.-only (check) · 🌐 remote · ⚠ link couldn't be verified recently · ⏳ listed for 60+ days.
A blank tag means the posting doesn't say. The grey line under a title is the sentence a tag came from.
**Posted** is the date the company posted the job. A date with * is when this board first saw it, because that company's job site doesn't give one. Companies sometimes re-post a job, which resets its date.

> 🛂❌ usually means no visa sponsorship later (for example H-1B). Unless the row also shows 🚫, CPT or OPT may still work. Check with the recruiter and your DSO.
