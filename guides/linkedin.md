# LinkedIn Setup

LinkedIn is where professionals and recruiters find each other. For a freshman it matters less than your classes and projects, so spend about an hour setting it up and then keep it updated.

## Profile checklist

- [ ] **Photo.** Face in good light, plain background, shoulders up. A phone photo is fine.
- [ ] **Name.** The same name as on your resume.
- [ ] **Location.** Your city, or the city you want to work in.
- [ ] **Custom URL.** Change it to `linkedin.com/in/yourname` in settings.

## Headline

The line under your name. Without one, LinkedIn shows only "Student".

> [Year] [Major] Student at [School] | [Skill] | [Skill]

Optional: add "Seeking Summer 20XX Internship" once you're actually applying.

## About section

3 or 4 short sentences: who you are (year, major), what you're learning or building, what you're looking for, and your email.

## Fill in the rest

- **Education:** school, major, expected graduation date.
- **Experience:** any job counts, even non-tech. One or two bullets each.
- **Projects:** each with a one-line description and the GitHub link.
- **Skills:** 5 to 10 you have really used.
- **Clubs and leadership:** ACM, other clubs, events you organized.
- **Languages:** list them if you speak more than one.

## Connecting

Connect with people you actually know or have talked to: classmates, club members, professors, people you met at events or career fairs (send the request the same day).

**Alumni from your school are your best contacts.** Search your school's page, then "People", and filter by company. Ask a professor or club officer if they know alumni in tech.

Note template (keep it short):

> Hi [Name], I'm a [year] CS student at [School] and saw you studied there too. I'm interested in [field]. Could I ask you one quick question about how you got started at [Company]?

Ask one specific question first. If they reply, then ask for a 15-minute chat. Many people won't answer; that's normal.

After you apply to a company, it's fine to message the recruiter or an alum there with one line saying you applied and why you're interested.
