# Student Guides

Short, practical guides for computer science and computer engineering students, written for freshmen and sophomores who have never had an internship and don't know where to start.

**You will not do all of this, and you don't need to.** Your classes come first. Pick one or two things a semester. Most people around you are starting from zero too.

## Year 1: start here

1. **Go to class and go to office hours.** Doing your own work in your first CS classes is the base for everything else.
2. **Join your school's ACM chapter or CS club** and go to one meeting ([networking](networking.md)).
3. **Pick one small [project](projects.md)** and build it with a friend.
4. **Put it on [GitHub](github.md)** with a clear README.
5. **Write a one-page [resume](resume.md)** and ask an upperclassman or your career center to look at it.

That's enough for year 1. If you have time left, set up [LinkedIn](linkedin.md) and read the [4-year timeline](timeline.md).

F-1 student? Read the [note for F-1 students](timeline.md#note-for-f-1-students) before you accept any job, even on campus.

## Year 2

Take Data Structures seriously, build one bigger [project](projects.md) with a team, and start [applying](applying.md) for internships in late summer (July to September for the next summer). After Data Structures, start [coding practice](timeline.md#coding-interview-practice).

Looking for clubs, programs, free courses, or project ideas? See [resources](resources.md).
