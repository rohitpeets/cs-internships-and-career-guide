# Resources

Too many lists make it hard to start. Begin with the 15 picks below. Use the groups after that when they fit your situation.

*Last checked: November 2026. Programs, dates, and prices change, so confirm on each site.*

## Top picks by need

**Start learning**
- [CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/): Harvard's free intro to CS. The best place to begin if you have never coded.
- [The Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu): the terminal, Git, and tools your classes skip.

**Build**
- [GitHub Skills](https://skills.github.com): free hands-on Git and GitHub lessons.
- [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com): free hosting for a static website.

**Get experience**
- [Forage](https://www.theforage.com): free virtual job simulations.
- [Parker Dewey](https://www.parkerdewey.com): short paid micro-internships.
- [Extern](https://www.extern.com): remote projects with companies. Free first-week trial, then a paid membership.

**Find your people**
- [ACM](https://www.acm.org): student chapters and contests. Join your school's.
- [CodePath](https://www.codepath.org): free courses for college students, with company connections.

**Practice interviews**
- [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io): coding problem lists with video explanations.
- [Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org): free guides to coding and behavioral rounds.
- [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com): free peer mock interviews with other students.

**More job lists**
- [Simplify's internship list](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships): a community-kept list of postings.
- [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com) (through your school) and your career center.
- [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov): federal jobs. See [government](government.md).

**Money**
- [DoD SMART](https://www.smartscholarship.org) and [CyberCorps SFS](https://www.sfs.opm.gov): scholarships that lead to government jobs. See [government](government.md#programs-to-know).
- Ask your financial aid office, your department, and your campus student government about travel grants and emergency funds.

## By situation

### Data and ML
- [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn): free short courses on Python, pandas, and more.
- [Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn): free courses on language models and other machine learning.
- [Break Through Tech](https://breakthroughtech.org): free AI/ML program for undergraduates from underrepresented groups.

### Computer engineering and hardware
- [Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org): build a computer from logic gates up.
- [HDLBits](https://hdlbits.01xz.net/wiki/Main_Page): Verilog practice, one small circuit at a time.
- Your school's IEEE student branch and robotics club.
- More in [tracks](tracks.md#embedded-and-firmware).

### Cybersecurity
- [picoCTF](https://picoctf.org): Carnegie Mellon's free security learning site, with beginner challenges.
- [OverTheWire](https://overthewire.org/wargames/): Linux and security games.
- [National Cyber League](https://www.nationalcyberleague.org): online competition for students.
- [CyberSeek](https://www.cyberseek.org): a map of cybersecurity jobs and career paths.
- More in [tracks](tracks.md#security).

### First-generation students
- Ask whether your school has a first-generation student office or a federal TRIO Student Support Services program. TRIO serves low-income and first-generation students and students with disabilities, and you must be accepted by a funded program near you. See the [U.S. Department of Education TRIO page](https://www2.ed.gov/about/offices/list/ope/trio/index.html).
- Your career center, and the referral steps in [applying](applying.md).

### Women and underrepresented groups
Join one or two. Many give free conference trips, mentors, and recruiter connections.
- [ColorStack](https://www.colorstack.org): Black and Latinx CS students.
- [NSBE](https://www.nsbe.org) and [SHPE](https://www.shpe.org): Black and Hispanic engineers; big national conventions and career fairs.
- [SWE](https://swe.org) and [Rewriting the Code](https://rewritingthecode.org): women in engineering and tech.
- [AnitaB.org](https://anitab.org): women in tech; runs the Grace Hopper Celebration.
- [Out in Tech](https://outintech.com) and [oSTEM](https://ostem.org): LGBTQ+ people in tech and STEM.
- [AISES](https://www.aises.org): Indigenous students in STEM.
- [Code2040](https://www.code2040.org) and [SEO Tech Developer](https://tech.seo-usa.org): career programs for students from underrepresented groups.
- Women Who Code closed in 2024. Use the groups above instead.

### Wellbeing
Job searching is stressful. Rejections are normal and say little about you.
- Your campus counseling center usually has free sessions for students. Look on your school's site or ask at the student health office.
- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org): call or text 988, or chat online, any time. Free and private. It is for any kind of emotional distress, not only emergencies.
- If you are in danger now, call 911.

### Funding conference trips
- [Grace Hopper Celebration](https://ghc.anitab.org) and [Tapia Conference](https://tapiaconference.cmd-it.org): student-friendly conferences. Check each site and its mailing list for student travel support.
- Ask your department, your ACM chapter, and student government for travel money. Say what you will learn and how you will share it with others at school.
- Many conferences have volunteer or student-rate options. Ask the organizers.
